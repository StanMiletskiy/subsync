About translation quality

Cueframe joins lines that continue the same thought, translates the phrase and fits it back into the original subtitle lines. It indexes the whole file to find related dialogue, including later mentions of names and expressions. The context available to the model depends on the language pair. Review ambiguous speakers, idioms and scene changes before publishing. Timestamps, line breaks and formatting stay intact. Unicode SRTs keep their encoding. Older encodings export as UTF-8.
