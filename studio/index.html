    Working with a movie or a different cut?

    Choose a timed SRT, an embedded text track in an MKV or MP4 movie, or a soundtrack your browser can decode. For other audio formats, extract the dialogue track as a mono WAV. With FFmpeg installed, this command selects the first audio track. Choose the audio track that matches your film.

    ffmpeg -i "movie.mkv" -map 0:a:0 -ac 1 -ar 16000 -c:a pcm_s16le "reference.wav"

    Automatic alignment compares dialogue timing across the film and applies timing changes it can match. When the correction is ready, select Export SRT to save it. If the overall match is too weak, choose a different reference or use manual anchors. For a cut between cues, place a pair early in the film, one just before the cut, one just after it, and one near the end. Lines missing from the release need editing separately.
